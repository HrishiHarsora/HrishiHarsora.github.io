I am a senior undergraduate at IIT Gandhinagar studying materials science in the context of micro/nanofabrication of logic and memory.

Over the course of the past ~3 years, I have used several modelling approaches in different contexts: from using DFT for 2D materials and their heterostructures to MD for thin film deposition and also some CFD. However, I have spent most of my time developing a probabilistic framework for modelling thin film deposition (paper coming soon).

Currently, I am a ==visiting research fellow at Caltech==, working with thermal and electron based ALD systems under Prof. Austin Minnich (Applied Physics). I am also working on extending the probabilistic framework to the ALD systems here.

## An Overambitious Goal

Working on a (possibly agentic) closed-loop framework which models the deposition process from first principles (Probably KMC), propagates it into a physics-based model of the resulting device (Multiferroics sound cool), and grounds both against static and dynamic characterization — making device performance a function of the process parameters.
